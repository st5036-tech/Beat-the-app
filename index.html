How To Play The Game

Grammar Rules Guide

• CAN / CAN'T: Present abilities & inabilities ("I can swim now, but I can't fly.")
• COULD / COULDN'T: Past abilities & inabilities ("When I was five, I could ride a bike.")
• REQUESTS: Use "Could you...?" for extra polite requests, and "Can you...?" for informal ones.
